## Copy choices worth a glance

- Women's health items are written as counseling *support through* these
  experiences, with a closing line that counseling works alongside medical
  care. Kelly is not a medical provider; the copy should never read as
  treating cancer or prescribing HRT.
- Puberty is listed with "(ages 16+)" to match the site's stated minimum age.
- Page `<title>` tags now list all five states and are long (~75–80 chars).
  Google will truncate them in results; the brand comes after the states so
  the states are what survives. If you'd rather keep titles short, change
  `LICENSED_STATES_SHORT` usages in the `title` fields to a fixed string.
- The "Which states" FAQ tells visitors they must be *physically located* in a
  licensed state during the session. That's the standard telehealth rule and
  worth keeping.
